01
Contract — Indent to Dispatch
A site engineer messages "hi create contract", and ZoAi opens a booking form with a quantity box per concrete grade (M-20 to M-40). Hindustan RMC confirms the draft in SellerHub, issuing a contract — quantity held at locked per-cum rates for a validity window. Every pour against it becomes an indent, raised either by the buyer in DM or by a sales rep in the internal ops group. Both routes converge on the same review page, which auto-validates the quantity against the remaining balance, posts the confirmed indent to the ERP, and ends with a dispatch note carrying the delivery challan — the 1-hour delivery promise, kept on WhatsApp.
