⚠ No WhatsApp Business Templates in Groups:
WhatsApp Business API templates (Marketing / Utility / Authentication) cannot be sent to groups. Groups only support plain session messages (text, media, documents). There are no Quick Reply buttons, CTA buttons, or structured template components in group messages. All group messages are plain conversational text with optional rich media (images, PDFs, voice notes).
ZoTok Use Case — Retailer Partner Hubs:
ZoTok uses WhatsApp Groups to create Retailer Partner Hubs — a shared space where the distributor's sales team (multiple members) and the retailer communicate together. The group acts as a persistent order room: any member can post updates, share invoices (as PDFs), or send voice notes. ZoTok's AI bot participates in the group and responds to queries from any member.
